The Scratch Card Reward Program offers an exciting opportunity for customers to earn rewards with every transaction. Each transaction qualifies for a scratch card that contains reward PINs, which are valued at ₹10 each. As a promotional offer, customers purchasing PINs will receive additional bonus PINs. For instance, customers earn ₹10 worth of credit for every ₹100 transferred, enabling them to purchase PINs. For a transfer amount of ₹1000, customers would receive 10 PINs, each valued at ₹10. These PINs are subject to availability and provided based on customer interest. Participation in the program is entirely optional, allowing customers to choose whether to purchase PINs depending on their transaction value and preference.
